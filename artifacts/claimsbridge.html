OCR reads pixels, not meaning. It cannot know rn=m or cl=d.

Form Semantics
OCR returns text literally. It cannot interpret meaning.

"02 23 92"→"02/23/1992"

Backed by Research
Peer-reviewed evidence from 2024–2025 confirms every design decision
📖 “OCR Post-Correction with LLMs: No Free Lunches”
Kanerva et al. — RESOURCEFUL @ ACL 2025
Without proper prompting, LLMs degrade CER by −57% to −74%. With correct config, English OCR improves.
→ Why prompt engineering is critical
arxiv.org/abs/2502.01205
📖 “KAP: MLLM-assisted OCR Text Enhancement”
Hsu et al. — arXiv 2025
MLLM post-processing improves retrieval by +12–18 MRR points over OCR alone.
→ Why LLM adds value on top of OCR
arxiv.org/abs/2503.08452
📖 “Hybrid OCR-LLM Framework”
Wang & Shen — arXiv, Oct 2025
Selective LLM on OCR output: F1 = 0.997. 54x gain vs pure multimodal.
→ Why OCR first, LLM second
arxiv.org/abs/2510.10138
📖 Few-Shot NER + Validation Agent
Shyr et al. 2023 • Toma et al. (WangLab) 2024
Few-shot: +25% F1 (0.472→0.591). DSPy validation: +8.4pp. Both stack.
→ Why we add examples + validation
