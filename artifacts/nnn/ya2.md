# Yandex

**Yandex** (Яндекс) is a Russian multinational technology company founded in 1997 by Arkady Volozh and Ilya Segalovich. It is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands (with major operations historically based in Moscow, Russia).

## Overview

Yandex is often referred to as the **"Google of Russia"** — it is the largest search engine in Russia and one of the largest internet companies in Europe. The company operates across a wide range of technology and internet services.

## Core Products & Services

- **Yandex Search** — The dominant search engine in Russia, holding over 60% market share domestically.
- **Yandex Browser** — A Chromium-based web browser with built-in security and AI features.
- **Yandex Maps** — A mapping and navigation service widely used across Russia and CIS countries.
- **Yandex Taxi / Yandex Go** — A ride-hailing and mobility super-app, similar to Uber.
- **Yandex Delivery** — Food and goods delivery service.
- **Yandex Music** — A music streaming platform.
- **Yandex Cloud** — A cloud computing platform offering IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS solutions.
- **Alice (Алиса)** — Yandex's AI-powered voice assistant.
- **YandexGPT** — A large language model (LLM) developed by Yandex.

## Recent Developments

- In 2022–2023, following geopolitical events related to the Russia-Ukraine war, Yandex underwent significant restructuring.
- The company split into two entities: the **international technology businesses** (retained under the Yandex brand in the Netherlands) and the **Russian operations**, which were rebranded.
- Yandex's Russian assets were sold to a consortium of Russian investors in 2024.

## Significance

Yandex is notable for being one of the few technology companies outside the US and China to build a comprehensive ecosystem of internet services — competing successfully with Google, Uber, Amazon, and others within its home market.
